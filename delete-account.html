YOUR DATA · YOUR CHOICE

Delete your POLIKRATOS account

You can request permanent deletion of your POLIKRATOS account and associated data without reinstalling the app.

In the game, open your profile and select Delete Account. Alternatively, use the email process below.

  1. Email us from the address connected to your account.
  2. Use the subject POLIKRATOS Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your display name. Never send your password.
  4. Complete account-ownership verification if requested.
Request deletion by email

Requests are processed without undue delay, normally within 30 days after verification.

What will be deleted

The deletion process removes your authentication account, account-linked push tokens and Wallet records, replaces your display name with “Deleted Player” and clears your avatar. Shared match history and results may remain linked to an internal player reference to preserve other players’ records. It does not erase every shared record or guarantee irreversible anonymisation.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or protection of other players. Retained information will be restricted to those purposes and kept only as long as necessary.

Purchases and subscriptions

Deletion removes account-linked Wallet balances and access records and can prevent access to purchased content. Contact support first if you need help with entitlements. Deletion does not cancel a subscription or automatically request a refund. Cancel renewals separately through Google Play or the store where you subscribed. Store purchase records and any legally required financial records are separate from your game profile. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Delete specific data without closing your account

To remove your uploaded profile photo, open Profile → Edit Name & Photo → Remove → Save. Your account and game progress remain. The saved photo is removed from your profile and its stored file is deleted after a successful update; cached copies may take time to expire.

For another data-deletion request, such as feedback you submitted, email info@polikratos.com from your account address with subject POLIKRATOS Data Deletion Request. Identify the data to remove; never send your password. We may verify ownership. Requests are normally handled within 30 days after verification. Unrelated account data remains, and the shared-history, legal and security retention limits above still apply. We will explain any data that cannot be deleted and why.

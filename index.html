About Polikratos

An Ancient Greek strategy card game built around tactical card decisions, city and faction sets, and rival alliances. Fair Play & Virtual Currency

POLIKRATOS is a strategy-based card game. It does not offer real-money gambling, wagering, cash betting, cash withdrawals or prizes of real-world monetary value.

Ⅰ

Inside the match

Money cards, treasury and tribute are gameplay resources used during a match. They are separate from your Drachma Wallet: paying tribute moves match resources, never purchased Wallet credits.

Ⅱ

Your Drachma Wallet

Drachmas are account-bound virtual credits for eligible in-game purchases, such as cosmetic boards and ad-free access. They cannot be transferred to other players, wagered on match outcomes, won from another player's Wallet or exchanged for cash.

Ⅲ

Optional purchases

Ad Free and Polikratos Plus subscriptions, Lifetime Access and cosmetic purchases do not provide competitive gameplay advantages. Drachma packs are optional one-time purchases where available. Current prices and access periods appear in the game and store checkout.

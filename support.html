POLIKRATOS Support

For technical help, account questions, privacy requests or gameplay feedback, contact the developer.

EMAILinfo@polikratos.comWe aim to respond within 5 business days.

Account access

Forgot your password? Reset your password, then return to the game and sign in. For a new account, follow the confirmation email first. If it has expired, use “Resend confirmation email” on the game's sign-in screen.

Include in your message

  • Your display name and device model.
  • The game mode and what happened.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.
  • Never include your password or authentication code.

Privacy and account controls

Read the Privacy Policy or use the dedicated Delete Account page.

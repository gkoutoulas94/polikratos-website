POLIKRATOS LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 August 2026 · Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Georgios Koutoulas (“we”, “us” or “the Developer”) processes information when you use the POLIKRATOS mobile game, website and related online services (together, the “Services”). For privacy questions, contact info@polikratos.com.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, we may process your email address, authentication provider identifier, display name, account identifier, profile image URL, sign-in method and account creation/update timestamps. Email/password and Google Sign-In are supported. Google authenticates you and supplies account information when you choose Google Sign-In. Apple sign-in is relevant only on supported iOS versions where offered.

You may optionally choose a profile photo from your device. The app crops and resizes it, removes image metadata and uploads a JPEG to our Supabase profile-photo storage. The selected photo is public: other players and anyone with its image URL can view it. You can replace or remove it from your profile. Account deletion also removes uploaded profile photos; cached copies may take time to expire.

Gameplay and multiplayer information

We process room membership, player seat, match identifiers, turns, game actions, match state, winners, timeouts, forfeits, match history, Ranked rating (ELO), wins and games played. Display names, avatars and leaderboard statistics may be visible to other authenticated players. Social and progression features also process friend requests, room invitations, blocked-player relationships, player reports (including any details you submit), achievements, mission progress and season rankings to deliver those features and investigate abuse.

Technical and security information

Our hosting and authentication provider may process IP addresses, timestamps, request metadata and diagnostic/security logs needed to deliver and protect online services. We do not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or broad access to your files. The optional profile-photo picker only gives the app access to the image you select.

Information stored on your device

Music volume, mute status, selected cosmetic board and similar preferences are stored locally. Local gameplay reminders may be generated on your device. Local-only data is not transmitted merely because it is stored on your device.

Push notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging provides remote gameplay notifications. The app registers a device push token, platform and update timestamp in Supabase against your signed-in account to route notifications such as a match-found alert. Google processes the token and delivery data. You can control notification display in device settings; disabling display does not itself delete an existing token. Account deletion removes account-linked push tokens.

Website requests

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives network request information such as your IP address and browser metadata. Website hosting processes requests to serve pages. The public information pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts. Support emails contain the information you send; never send passwords or payment-card details.

2. How we use information

  • Provide account creation, authentication and account recovery.
  • Operate Local, Private and Ranked gameplay, rooms, turns and leaderboards.
  • Maintain fair play, validate game actions and prevent abuse or cheating.
  • Send gameplay notifications you have permitted.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve the Services.
  • Respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.

3. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, processing is based on performance of our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the game, your consent where required (including notification permission), and compliance with legal obligations.

Account, multiplayer and purchase verification data support delivery of the service and purchases you request. Security, abuse reports and fraud checks support our legitimate interests in protecting players and transactions. Support records and financial records may also be processed to meet legal obligations. Where consent is legally required for device storage, analytics or advertising, that requirement applies independently of notification permission or acceptance of these Terms; an opt-out control alone is not a statement that prior consent was obtained.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase for authentication, database, realtime multiplayer and server functions. Hostinger provides website and email hosting. Google processes information for sign-in, Firebase services, advertising and Google Play billing. Apple processes information only when its services are offered and used on a supported platform. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users and the Services, or as part of a lawful business reorganisation. We do not sell personal information. Advertising-related processing by Google is described below and is subject to your privacy choices.

5. Advertising, analytics and payments

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and improve its balance, reliability and user experience. Analytics may include app interactions and technical information such as selected game mode, tutorial progress, match duration and outcome, aggregated game-action and card categories, matchmaking activity, board previews and purchase-flow events, together with device or app-instance information automatically processed by Firebase. Our custom analytics events do not include names, email addresses, private room codes, card copy identifiers or match identifiers. Advertising ID collection by Firebase Analytics is disabled. Analytics collection is enabled by default in the app and can be disabled in Settings using the “anonymous analytics” control. Device and app-instance identifiers mean this data should not be understood as necessarily anonymous. We do not use analytics for cross-context behavioural advertising or sell analytics data. Purchases are processed by Google Play; payment-card details are not provided to us.

Advertising and Drachma wallet

Google AdMob provides ads when enabled and available, including interstitial ads and optional rewarded offers after eligible completed games. Ad-free access suppresses automatic interstitial ads; an ad-free player may still explicitly choose a rewarded offer. Wallet earning is not currently enabled in production. The reward verification described below applies when that feature is enabled. The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request consent information and display a consent form when required by that platform. Advertising privacy choices are available in Settings; available choices depend on your region and Google’s configuration. An ad request proceeds only when the consent SDK permits it. Depending on your settings and choices, Google AdMob processes information such as IP addresses (which may be used to infer approximate location), device identifiers (including the advertising identifier where available and permitted), ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. See Google Privacy Policy. To verify a reward, we send an account identifier and a reward-session identifier to Google and receive signed confirmation containing the ad unit, transaction identifier and event timestamp. We keep account-linked balances, reward sessions, credit and redemption records, and unlocked access in Supabase. Drachma are separate from in-match money and cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash.

Subscriptions and other store purchases

For Ad Free, Polikratos Plus, Lifetime Access and cosmetic purchases, the app and verification server process your account identifier, product identifier, purchase token or receipt, transaction reference, subscription expiry and entitlement status. Google Play purchase verification and store notifications help deliver or restore access and handle renewals, cancellations, refunds and revocations. Payment-card details are handled by the store, not supplied to us.

Drachma purchases

When enabled, optional Drachma packs are repeatable one-time purchases through Google Play. We send an obfuscated account identifier to Google to bind purchases to the correct game account. Our server verifies the purchase with Google and stores its product, transaction identifier, hashed purchase reference, credited amount and any refund adjustment. We use these records to deliver purchases, prevent duplicate credits and process refunds. Purchased Drachma do not expire. A refund of already-spent Drachma can leave a negative wallet balance, which later credits offset.

Crash diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to investigate crashes and errors. Reports can contain stack traces, app version, operating-system and device information, timestamps and installation identifiers. Crash reporting is enabled by default and can be disabled separately in the app's Settings. We do not intentionally attach names, email addresses, private room codes or card hands to crash reports.

Google Play Games

On Android, Google Play Games may authenticate your Play Games profile and process its player identifier and display name. If you choose to synchronise achievements or submit scores, achievement unlocks and leaderboard scores are sent to Google. Your Play Games profile can differ from your POLIKRATOS account. Google processes this information under its privacy policy and your Play Games profile visibility settings.

Password recovery

Our password recovery page sends your email address to Supabase to deliver a reset link. The page uses a temporary authentication session to update your password. It does not run advertising or analytics scripts, and does not store that session persistently in the browser. After changing your password, return to the game and sign in again.

6. Retention

Account and profile information is retained while your account is active. Gameplay, ranking and security records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide multiplayer history, preserve leaderboard integrity, resolve disputes and prevent abuse. Wallet balances, sessions, purchase-credit records, redemption records and wallet access grants are linked to your account and deleted with it. Google retains its own purchase records under its policies. Store verification, refund and security records may be retained separately where needed for dispute handling, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Any financial records required by law are distinct from the game profile and retained only for the applicable purpose and period; this policy does not promise deletion of the store’s records. Retention of provider logs and diagnostics also depends on the relevant provider settings and policies. Local preferences remain until you clear app data or uninstall. We may retain limited records where required for security, fraud prevention or legal compliance.

7. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account from the in-game profile using Delete Account, or request deletion through our Delete Account page or by emailing info@polikratos.com. We may ask you to verify account ownership. The in-app deletion process removes authentication access and push tokens and replaces the profile name with “Deleted Player” while clearing its avatar. Shared match history and results may remain with an internal player reference to preserve other players’ records; this should not be understood as erasing every match record or guaranteeing irreversible anonymisation. Account-linked Wallet records are deleted with the authentication account. Email requests are handled without undue delay, normally within 30 days after ownership verification; any legally permitted extension will be explained. Limited records may be retained where required for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. Deleting an account does not cancel store subscriptions; cancel them separately through the store.

8. Security and international processing

We use access controls, authenticated connections, row-level database policies and encrypted network transport. No system is completely secure. Service providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA/UK. Where required, transfers must rely on an applicable adequacy decision or contractual safeguards. Contact us to request information about the safeguards relevant to your data. See the Supabase Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and Hostinger Privacy Policy.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, and may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Send requests to info@polikratos.com; we may verify ownership and will explain applicable exceptions. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can disable analytics and crash reporting separately and adjust music preferences in the app's Settings, and disable notifications in device settings. EEA/UK users may also complain to their local data-protection authority.

10. Children

POLIKRATOS is not directed to children under 13, or below the minimum digital-consent age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information improperly.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy as the Services evolve. The effective date above will change, and material changes may also be communicated in the app or through another appropriate channel.
